# H007 — RBI HaRBInger 2025 (4th RBI Global Hackathon) — TIER 2

- Organizer: Reserve Bank of India | Launched 23 Oct 2025; 496 teams from 15 countries; winners reported May 2026
- Theme: "Secure Banking: Powered by Identity, Integrity and Inclusivity"
- Prizes: ₹40L winner / ₹20L runner-up per problem statement; ₹20L best all-women team
- Winner sources: https://www.cryptotimes.io/2026/05/19/rbi-harbinger-2025-winners-feature-india-cbdc-tokenised-kyc-ai-fraud-tools/ ; https://www.psuconnect.in/bank-news/bank-of-baroda-wins-big-at-rbi-harbinger-2025-hackathon-with-two-major-awards

**Structural lesson (INFERENCE):** every HaRBInger winner is an incumbent institution or a funded startup, and *none* publish code. The winning artefact is a risk/identity decision surface with an explicit action taxonomy (block / approve / step-up), not a model score.
